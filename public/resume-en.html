01RESEARCH

STABLE

ICASSP 2027 · Under Review

Improving MLLM attribute binding with visual evidence

First author · Idea & method design · Implementation & experiments · Paper writing

  • Analyzed layer-wise visual evidence and binding decisions, then designed selective inference steering based on agreement between syntax and image attention. Calibration estimates steering directions without ground-truth labels; inference requires no weight updates and one forward pass per caption.
  • Evaluated Qwen2-VL and LLaVA-1.5 on four benchmarks. In the paper, LLaVA-1.5-7B ColorSwap Group accuracy increased from VQAScore’s 67.33% to 73.00%. On 100 ARO samples with LLaVA-1.5 and a TITAN RTX, STABLE took 0.60 s/sample—4.1× faster than CRG and 25.6× faster than COCO-Tree. Submitted to ICASSP 2027 on Sep 24, 2026; under review.
Multimodal LLM / Activation Steering / Training-free Inference
